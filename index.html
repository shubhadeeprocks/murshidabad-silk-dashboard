🏭 Production & Value Chain Gaps

As highlighted in the policy proposal, Murshidabad shows high cocoon dependency but low raw silk conversion due to disease risk and outdated reeling infrastructure.

  • Bi-voltine seed promotion
  • Automatic Reeling Machines (ARM)
  • District convergence monitoring
🧵 Loom Infrastructure Issues

Under-utilisation of looms is driven by migration, obsolete pit looms, and weak design-market linkage.

  • Modern loom subsidies
  • Design & jacquard training
  • Cluster-based production
💳 Credit & Financial Inclusion

Despite scheme availability, credit disbursement lags due to administrative and banking bottlenecks.

  • Block-level finance clinics
  • Real-time credit MIS
  • NPA resolution for cooperatives
🌍 Market & Export Strategy

The policy positions Murshidabad silk as a heritage luxury product requiring branding, GI enforcement, and digital platforms.

  • GI Tag & Silk Mark
  • Biswa Bangla branding
  • ONDC & e-commerce onboarding
