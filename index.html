🏭 Production & Administrative Insight

As highlighted in the policy proposal, Murshidabad’s silk sector shows high cocoon dependency but low raw silk conversion due to disease risk, outdated reeling units, and fragmented value chains.

  • Need for bi-voltine seed promotion
  • Automatic Reeling Machines (ARM)
  • District-led convergence monitoring

🧵 Loom Infrastructure Gap

The document identifies under-utilisation of looms caused by migration, obsolete pit looms, and lack of design integration.

  • Modern loom subsidy targeting youth
  • Design–market linkage via clusters
  • Role of District Collector as coordinator

💳 Credit Delivery & Policy Execution Gap

Despite scheme availability, disbursement lags indicate administrative friction between banks, cooperatives, and beneficiaries.

  • Block-level finance clinics
  • Real-time MIS monitoring
  • NPA resolution mechanisms

🌍 Market & Export Potential

The proposal positions Murshidabad silk as a heritage luxury product, requiring branding, GI enforcement, and digital platforms.

  • GI Tag & Silk Mark certification
  • ONDC & Biswa Bangla onboarding
  • Export facilitation cells
